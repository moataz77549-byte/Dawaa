# Scientific review policy

Status progression: draft → needs_review → approved → published, or withdrawn.
Publication permission and clinical review are separate. Nonclinical original
introductions visibly retain `needs_review`, with an explicit pending-clinical-
review notice. No fabricated reviewer or review timestamp is assigned.

Clinical/dosing material requires a named verified reviewer, a documented review
decision, a review date, source version and applicable region. The database rejects
published clinical content without these fields. A human pharmacist/qualified
reviewer must validate all clinical claims before release. Stage 1 has no dosing,
patient cases or automated treatment recommendations. Future source ingestion
must log attribution, rights and update/withdrawal events in audit records.

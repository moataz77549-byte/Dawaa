# Content and rights policy

Every original summary identifies its provenance, reference URL, license,
attribution, revision and publication/review state. The bundled seed consists of
newly authored introductory descriptions; linked NIH/FDA pages are for further
reading, not copied or relicensed content. None is a dosing instruction.

Only published content with explicitly allowed redistribution reaches the API.
Private/unlicensed sources and drafts are hidden. Original geometric assets are
CC0. Closed atlases and UpToDate/BNF/Lexicomp/Martindale/DrugBank content must not
be imported without verified redistribution permission. Source publication dates
stay unknown/null when not verified; fetch date is never clinical approval.

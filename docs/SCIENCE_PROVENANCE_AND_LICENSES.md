# Scientific provenance and licenses

A source record includes source/native IDs, canonical URL, fetched/source-update times, response ETag/Last-Modified where present, SHA-256, metadata projection, original language, license, permitted display mode and review state. A fact additionally carries section, provenance snapshot, evidence class, jurisdiction/population fields and separate reviewer/date/decision. Missing source/review dates stay null; fetch time is never presented as medical review time.

Imports are identity or bibliographic metadata only. FDA clinical sections are recorded as section names, presence flags and hashes, not copied dosage text. DailyMed stores label identity and official links. PubMed, Europe PMC and Crossref exclude abstracts/full text. MedlinePlus stores topic title and official URL, without copied summary. RxClass omits underlying vocabulary content pending reuse verification. No logos or sponsorship claims are used.

Offline export currently allowlists only non-proprietary RxNorm names and RxCUIs. The application's original explanatory wrapper and independent Arabic draft are CC-BY-4.0; source metadata retains the separately displayed NLM terms. This wrapper license does not relicense third-party data. Per-record offline permission, hash, source and license are mandatory. Export is reproducible from the committed verified projection and inspected by `knowledge_policy_lint.mjs`.

All translations and original educational cards remain needs_review. There are no active qualified reviewers and no published imported clinical/dosing facts. Source accessibility and bibliographic indexing never count as specialist approval. Future full-text imports require item-specific license evidence and a qualified reviewer, neither of which is fabricated here.
